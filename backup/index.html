A product backup you can read, download and trust.

Every night Ackly Backup saves your products, variants, prices, stock and metafields, and shows you exactly what changed since the day before. Download it all as a CSV whenever you want. Restore a product with a preview first and a check after.

What it keeps, every night

  • Products: title, description, vendor, type, tags, status, SEO fields
  • Every variant: options, price, compare-at price, SKU, barcode, stock per location, tracking
  • Product and variant metafields
  • 90 days of history, with what changed from one day to the next

Not yet: product images, themes, customers and orders. We say so up front so a restore never surprises you.

Restores you can check

Pick a day and a product. Ackly Backup shows you what will change before it writes anything. Stock is only restored if you ask for it, because yesterday's stock would undo today's sales. After the restore it reads the product back from Shopify and tells you if anything still differs.
