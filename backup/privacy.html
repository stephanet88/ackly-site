Ackly Backup privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026. Ackly is based in Luxembourg. Questions: hello@ackly.app.

What Ackly Backup reads

From Shopify: your products (title, description, vendor, type, tags, status, SEO fields), their variants (options, prices, SKU, barcode, stock per location, tracking), product and variant metafields, and your locations' names.

Ackly Backup does not read customer, order or payment data. It only writes to Shopify when you restore a product, and only after showing you a preview.

What we store and why

One copy of that product data per night, kept for 90 days, so you can see what changed, download it and restore it. Your shop domain and the access Shopify gives the app. We don't sell, share or advertise with any of it.

Where and how long

Backups are stored on our server in the European Union and deleted after 90 days. When you uninstall the app, Shopify sends us a deletion request 48 hours later and we delete all of your store's backups then.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or its deletion at any time by emailing hello@ackly.app. We answer within 30 days. You can also complain to Luxembourg's data protection authority (CNPD).

Service providers

Shopify, to read and restore your data with your permission; Tailscale, which carries traffic to our server.
