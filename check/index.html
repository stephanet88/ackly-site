Is your eBay out of sync with Shopify?

Drop in two files you already have. In a few seconds you'll see every eBay listing that's sold out in Shopify but still buyable, listed above your stock, linked to a SKU that doesn't exist, or priced differently. Free. It runs in your browser: your files never leave your computer.

Where exactly do I find these files?

Shopify: in your admin open Products, click Export, choose "All products" and "CSV for Excel" or "Plain CSV file". Shopify emails it if your catalog is large.

eBay: open Seller Hub, go to Reports, then Downloads, request an "Active listings" report and download the CSV once it's ready (usually a minute or two).

Listings are matched by SKU: Shopify's "Variant SKU" against eBay's "Custom label (SKU)".
