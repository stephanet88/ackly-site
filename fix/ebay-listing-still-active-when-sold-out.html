Sold out in Shopify, still live on eBay

The eBay listing didn't receive Shopify's zero. Most often it's unlinked, inventory isn't tracked on the variant, or the sync app's out-of-stock rule keeps listings alive.

Check yours in a minute. The free sync checker compares your Shopify export with your eBay listings report in your browser and lists every listing that doesn't match. Nothing is uploaded.

Why it happens

Out-of-stock control

Some setups keep a listing alive at zero or relist it automatically; check what yours does at zero.

Untracked inventory

With Track quantity off, Shopify never reports zero.

Unlinked listing

No link, no update.

How to fix it

  1. Run the free checker below to list every listing that's sold out in Shopify but still buyable on eBay.
  2. Zero or end them on eBay right away.
  3. Turn on Track quantity, relink the listings, and check your sync app's rule for what happens at zero stock.

It's the costliest drift: every order on one of these listings is a cancellation, a refund and a mark on your eBay seller record.
