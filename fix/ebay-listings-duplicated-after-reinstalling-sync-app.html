Your eBay listings are duplicated or no longer update after you reinstalled or switched a sync app

After reinstalling or switching a sync app, eBay listings can be duplicated or stop updating because the new install didn't recognize the existing ones.

Check yours in a minute. The free sync checker compares your Shopify export with your eBay listings report in your browser and lists every listing that doesn't match. Nothing is uploaded.

Why it happens

The new install didn't recognize your old listings

Some apps match existing eBay listings to Shopify products by SKU. If they can't match, they may create new listings or leave the old ones alone.

Missing or different SKUs

If the eBay Custom label is empty or differs from the Shopify Variant SKU, there is nothing to match on.

The old listings are still live

A listing the new app doesn't manage keeps its last quantity and price. It no longer follows Shopify, so it can oversell.

How to fix it

  1. Don't delete or end anything yet. Export your Shopify products and your active eBay listings report.
  2. Run the free checker. Rows saying the SKU matches nothing in Shopify are the stale or duplicate listings.
  3. For each one, relink first: make the eBay Custom label equal the Shopify Variant SKU. Ending a listing can lose its sales history and watchers, so end only true duplicates.
  4. Run the checker again until it comes back clean.
