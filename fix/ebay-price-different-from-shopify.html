Your eBay prices don't match Shopify

Prices drift when a price change in Shopify doesn't reach eBay, when a markup rule changed, or when someone edited the price on eBay directly and the next sync didn't overwrite it.

Check yours in a minute. The free sync checker compares your Shopify export with your eBay listings report in your browser and lists every listing that doesn't match. Nothing is uploaded.

Why it happens

Price sync turned off

Many sync apps sync quantity by default but price only if you enable it.

Markup rules

A percentage markup for eBay fees makes every price different on purpose; a changed rule makes some of them wrong.

Manual edits on eBay

A price edited on eBay can stay until the next full sync, or forever if price sync is off.

How to fix it

  1. Run the free checker below: "Price differs from Shopify" rows show both prices side by side.
  2. Decide whether eBay should equal Shopify or follow a markup, then set that once in your sync app.
  3. Turn price sync on and re-run the checker.

If you add a markup on eBay to cover fees, set it once in your sync app so every listing follows the same rule; anything else is drift.
