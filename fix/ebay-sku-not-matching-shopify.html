Your eBay SKUs don't match Shopify

Sync apps match eBay listings to Shopify by SKU. If eBay's Custom label differs from Shopify's Variant SKU by even one character, that listing is never updated again.

Check yours in a minute. The free sync checker compares your Shopify export with your eBay listings report in your browser and lists every listing that doesn't match. Nothing is uploaded.

Why it happens

Parent SKU on eBay, variant SKUs in Shopify

Multi-variation items often carry the parent product's SKU on eBay while Shopify has one SKU per size and colour.

Case and spaces

"ab-1 " and "AB-1" look the same to you and different to most sync apps.

Old SKUs after a rename

Renaming SKUs in Shopify without updating eBay leaves every old listing orphaned.

Variants with no SKU

A variant without a SKU can't be matched at all.

How to fix it

  1. Run the free checker below with your two exports: every "SKU matches nothing in Shopify" row is an orphaned eBay listing.
  2. Pick one source of truth (Shopify) and copy each variant's SKU exactly into eBay's Custom label.
  3. Give every variant you sell a SKU, then relink listings in your sync app.
  4. Re-run the checker until no unlinked rows remain.

Unlinked or renamed SKUs are one of the most common causes merchants describe when a sync app "stops updating" a listing.
