Your eBay variations don't match your Shopify variants

When sizes or colors don't line up between eBay and Shopify, stock and prices can't sync correctly for those options.

Check yours in a minute. The free sync checker compares your Shopify export with your eBay listings report in your browser and lists every listing that doesn't match. Nothing is uploaded.

Why it happens

Variants without a unique SKU

If several Shopify variants share one SKU, or have none, a sync app can't tell which eBay variation each one belongs to.

Different labels per variation

The eBay variation may carry a Custom label that differs from the Shopify variant SKU, so the two never match.

Renamed or reordered options

Changing an option name or value on one side can break the link on the other.

How to fix it

  1. Give every Shopify variant its own unique SKU.
  2. Make each eBay variation's Custom label equal that variant SKU.
  3. Run the free checker. A variation whose SKU matches nothing in Shopify is the one that won't sync.
  4. After fixing links, re-run the checker to confirm stock and prices now line up per option.
