Marketplace Connect isn't syncing your inventory to eBay

Usually it's one of four things: the listing isn't linked to the right Shopify variant, the SKUs differ by a character, inventory tracking is off on the product, or the eBay order never imported (Marketplace Connect lowers Shopify stock by importing the eBay order).

Check yours in a minute. The free sync checker compares your Shopify export with your eBay listings report in your browser and lists every listing that doesn't match. Nothing is uploaded.

Why it happens

The listing lost its link

eBay listings can show as matched, then drop to unmatched after edits, relists or variant changes. An unlinked listing keeps its old quantity forever.

SKUs differ by one character

A trailing space, a different case or the parent SKU instead of the variant's (TEE instead of TEE-RED-M) is enough to break matching.

Inventory isn't tracked in Shopify

If "Track quantity" is off on the variant, there is no number to send, so eBay keeps whatever it had.

The eBay order didn't import

Stock only drops in Shopify when the eBay order is created there. A failed order import means Shopify, and then every other channel, still shows the unit as available.

How to fix it

  1. Export your Shopify products and your eBay active listings, and run them through the free checker below: it lists every listing whose stock or price doesn't match, and every SKU that matches nothing.
  2. For each unlinked SKU, make it identical on both sides (copy it from Shopify into eBay's Custom label), then run Link listings again in Marketplace Connect.
  3. Turn on Track quantity for every variant you sell on eBay and set real counts.
  4. In Marketplace Connect, open Orders and look for failed eBay imports; fix the reason shown (often an unlinked or deleted product) and retry them.
  5. Run the checker again. Zero red rows means the channels agree.
