Why it happens

Sync delay

Apps that sync on a schedule leave a window where both channels show the same last unit.

Unlinked listing

If the eBay listing isn't linked to the Shopify variant, the Shopify sale never reaches it.

One-way sync

Some setups push Shopify to eBay but never bring eBay orders back, so counts drift in one direction.

No safety buffer

Selling your very last unit on every channel at once guarantees the occasional double sale.

How to fix it

  1. Run the free checker below: red "Sold out in Shopify, still buyable on eBay" rows are the listings that can oversell right now.
  2. End or zero those listings first, then fix their links or SKUs.
  3. Check your sync app's order import: eBay sales must come back into Shopify to lower stock.
  4. For fast movers, keep a small buffer (list a quantity cap on eBay) so a delayed sync can't sell what you don't have.
