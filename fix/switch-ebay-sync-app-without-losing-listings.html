Why it happens

What links your listings

Most setups link an eBay listing to a Shopify variant through the SKU or the eBay Custom label. Write down which one yours uses.

What the old app does when you remove it

Depending on the app, uninstalling can leave listings live and unmanaged, or end them. Check your app's documentation before you uninstall.

The gap between two apps

While neither app manages a listing, stock and prices can drift. That is when oversells happen.

How to fix it

  1. Export your Shopify products and your active eBay listings report before changing anything, and keep both files.
  2. Check that every variant has a unique SKU that equals its eBay Custom label.
  3. Switch during a quiet period, and keep the old app installed until the new one shows your listings as linked.
  4. After the switch, run the free checker. Any row it flags is a listing that drifted or was left behind. Fix those first.
