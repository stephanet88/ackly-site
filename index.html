Shopify apps that show their work.

Small apps for problems Shopify merchants describe in their own reviews, each one telling you exactly what it checked and what it did.

  • Ackly Watch: checks your eBay listings against Shopify every 5 minutes and tells you when they drift. Early access.
  • Ackly Backup: nightly product backup you can read, download as a CSV and restore with a check. Early access.
  • Ackly Send: WhatsApp order messages with proof they arrived. Founding list.
  • Free eBay sync checker: compare your eBay and Shopify exports in your browser. Files never leave your computer.

Ackly, made in Luxembourg.
