Questions

Is it live?

Not yet. We're building it for the stores on the founding list and launching on the Shopify App Store in early 2027. Join the list and we'll email you once, when you can install it.

Can I keep using WhatsApp on my phone?

Yes. Meta's coexistence setup lets the WhatsApp Business app and Ackly Send share the same number, so you keep your chats and contacts.

Could my number get banned?

Numbers get restricted when people who never asked for messages report them. Ackly Send only messages customers who opted in at checkout, paces large sends, and checks your number's quality rating every day, so you're warned while there's still time to act.

What does Meta charge?

It depends on the customer's country and the type of message; order updates cost less than marketing. You'll see the exact fee on every line of your log and your bill.

Which languages?

Any language WhatsApp supports. You write or approve each template, so messages sound like your store, not like a robot.
