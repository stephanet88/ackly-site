Questions

Is it live?

Yes, for eBay. Etsy comes next. Leave your email and we set up your free 2-week check personally, usually the same day.

Does it replace my sync app?

No. Your sync app keeps doing the syncing. Ackly Watch only checks its work and tells you when it slipped.

Which sync apps does it work with?

Any of them, including Shopify Marketplace Connect, because it compares the marketplaces with Shopify directly rather than reading the sync app.

Can it change my listings?

No. It only reads. It never edits prices, titles or stock on eBay or in Shopify.

What if a check fails?

You're told. A marketplace that can't be reached is shown as unknown, never as a match, so silence always means checked and fine.
