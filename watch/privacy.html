Ackly Watch privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026. Ackly is based in Luxembourg. Questions: hello@ackly.app.

What Ackly Watch reads

From Shopify: your store's domain and currency, and for each product variant its SKU, price, stock quantity and whether stock is tracked. From eBay, once you connect it: your active listings' item ID, SKU, quantity and price. You give us an email address for alerts if you want them.

Ackly Watch does not read or store customer, order or payment data. It never writes to Shopify or eBay.

What we store and why

Your shop domain, your eBay connection token (to read your listings), your alert email, your channel rules and the latest check's findings. We use them only to compare your listings with Shopify and tell you what drifted. We don't sell, share or advertise with any of it.

Where and how long

Data is stored on our server in the European Union. When you uninstall the app, we delete your store's data straight away; Shopify's own deletion request removes anything left within 48 hours.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or its deletion at any time by emailing hello@ackly.app. We answer within 30 days. You can also complain to Luxembourg's data protection authority (CNPD).

Service providers

Shopify and eBay, to read your data with your permission; Tailscale, which carries traffic to our server; our email provider, to send alerts.
